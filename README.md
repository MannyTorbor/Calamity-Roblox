# Calamity

Calamity is an action RPG inspired by the classic Roblox game **Reality**.

In a world where Guest 0 has finally had enough, he unleashes a calamity that causes superpowers to appear within those capable of handling them. You must master your abilities, stop Guest 0 from taking over the world, and prevent him from bending reality to his will.

**Development status:** Calamity is currently in development and is not publicly available to play.

## Gameplay Systems

- Fast-paced melee combat and dashing
- Four elemental powers: Ice, Fire, Void, and Thunder
- Ability cooldowns and energy-cost balancing
- XP and leveling systems
- In-game currency
- Four allocatable player stats
- NPC aggro detection and combat behavior
- NPC death and respawning
- Weighted Power Orb spawning with different rarity levels
- Client-server communication using Roblox RemoteEvents
